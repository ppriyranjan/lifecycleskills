# Lifecycle Marketing Campaign Templates

This document provides detailed templates for different types of lifecycle marketing campaigns. Match features to the appropriate campaign type based on their characteristics and goals.

## Campaign Type Overview

| Campaign Type | Best For | Primary Goal | Typical Timeline |
|--------------|----------|--------------|------------------|
| New Feature Launch | Brand new capabilities | Awareness + Trial | 2-4 weeks |
| Onboarding & Education | Complex features needing learning | Adoption + Success | 3-6 weeks |
| Re-engagement | Underutilized features | Activation + Habit | 2-3 weeks |
| Upgrade/Upsell | Premium/advanced features | Conversion + Revenue | Ongoing |
| Integration Announcement | Third-party integrations | Awareness + Setup | 2-3 weeks |
| Deprecation/Sunset | Feature transitions | Smooth migration | 6-12 weeks |

---

## 1. New Feature Launch Campaign

**When to use:** Announcing brand new capabilities that users couldn't do before.

**Campaign Goals:**
- Create awareness of the new feature
- Drive initial trial and adoption
- Generate excitement and word-of-mouth
- Establish the feature as part of the product value

### Template Structure

**Campaign Name:** "Introducing [Feature]: [One-line Value Prop]"

**Example:** "Introducing Team Analytics: See what's working at a glance"

### Channels & Tactics

**Week 1: Announcement**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "New: [Feature] is here"
  - Hero image showing the feature
  - 2-3 key benefits with visuals
  - Clear CTA: "Try it now"
  - Segment: All users or relevant segment

- **In-App:**
  - Modal or banner announcing the feature
  - Tooltip tour on first visit
  - Persistent navigation highlight for 2 weeks

- **Blog Post:**
  - Detailed announcement with use cases
  - Screenshots and/or demo video
  - Customer quote if available
  - SEO optimized for "[product] [feature]"

**Week 2: Educational Follow-up**
- **Email (to non-adopters):**
  - Subject: "3 ways to use [Feature]"
  - Short use case examples
  - Quick start guide link
  - Video tutorial if available

- **Social Media:**
  - Feature announcement post
  - Short demo video or GIF
  - Customer success story (if available)

- **Help Center:**
  - Getting started guide
  - FAQ document
  - Video walkthrough

**Week 3-4: Social Proof**
- **Email (to all users):**
  - Subject: "[Number] teams are already using [Feature]"
  - Usage statistics and early wins
  - Customer testimonial or case study
  - Reminder CTA

- **In-App (to non-adopters):**
  - Contextual prompt at relevant moment
  - "Others like you are using [Feature]"

### Targeting & Segmentation

**Primary Segment:** Users who would benefit most
- Identify by role, plan tier, or behavior
- Example: "Team admins on Pro plans"

**Secondary Segment:** Broad user base
- Awareness for future consideration
- Lower-touch communication

### Success Metrics

- **Awareness:** Email open rate, blog page views
- **Trial:** Feature activation rate within 7/14/30 days
- **Engagement:** Feature usage frequency and depth
- **Satisfaction:** NPS or feedback score from feature users

### Example Campaign

**Feature:** Advanced Team Analytics Dashboard

**Campaign:** "Introducing Team Analytics: Data-driven team management made easy"

**Timeline:**
- **Week 1:** Launch announcement
  - Email to team leads and admins on Pro/Enterprise
  - In-app modal with interactive tour
  - Blog post: "How [Company] teams use data to improve performance"

- **Week 2:** Education
  - Email: "5 insights you can unlock with Team Analytics"
  - Video: 2-minute dashboard walkthrough
  - Webinar invitation for live demo

- **Week 3:** Social proof
  - Email: "Join 500+ teams using Analytics"
  - Customer case study published
  - In-app prompt for non-adopters

**Success Metrics:**
- 40% of Pro/Enterprise team leads activate within 30 days
- 60% of activators return in week 2
- 4.5+ rating in feature feedback

## 2. Onboarding & Education Campaign

**When to use:** Features that require learning, setup, or behavioral change.

**Campaign Goals:**
- Reduce time-to-value
- Increase successful adoption
- Prevent abandonment or confusion
- Build user competence and confidence

### Template Structure

**Campaign Name:** "Get Started with [Feature] in [X] Steps"

**Example:** "Master automated workflows in 4 simple steps"

### Channels & Tactics

**Day 1: Welcome & Setup**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Welcome! Let's set up [Feature]"
  - Quick overview of benefits
  - Step 1: First action to take
  - Estimated time: "5 minutes to set up"

- **In-App:**
  - Setup wizard or guided flow
  - Progress indicators
  - Tooltips for each step

**Day 3-4: First Use Case**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Try this: [Specific use case]"
  - Step-by-step instructions with screenshots
  - Template or example to follow
  - Link to video tutorial

**Day 7: Best Practices**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Pro tips for [Feature]"
  - 3-4 expert tips from power users
  - Common mistakes to avoid
  - Link to advanced guides

**Day 14: Success Check-in**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "How's [Feature] working for you?"
  - Quick survey (1-2 questions)
  - Additional resources based on usage
  - Offer: "Need help? Book a call"

**Ongoing: Contextual Help**
- **In-App:**
  - Help center articles linked in-context
  - Empty state guides
  - Success patterns and examples

### Targeting & Segmentation

**Trigger:** User activates feature for the first time

**Segments:**
- **Engaged learners:** Clicked welcome email, watched video
- **Slow starters:** Activated but minimal usage
- **Abandoned:** Activated but no usage in 7 days

### Success Metrics

- **Completion:** % who complete setup/onboarding
- **Time-to-value:** Days until first meaningful use
- **Sustained usage:** % still using after 30 days
- **Competence:** % who use advanced capabilities

### Example Campaign

**Feature:** Automated Workflow Builder

**Campaign:** "Build your first workflow in 4 steps"

**Timeline:**
- **Day 1:** Welcome email with workflow templates
- **Day 3:** Email tutorial: "Create an approval workflow"
- **Day 7:** Email: "5 workflows every team needs"
- **Day 10:** Video: "Advanced workflow patterns"
- **Day 14:** Success check-in email with offer to book demo

**In-App:**
- Interactive workflow wizard
- Template library with pre-built workflows
- Inline help and examples

**Success Metrics:**
- 70% complete first workflow within 7 days
- 50% create second workflow within 30 days
- 80% of workflow creators still active at day 30

## 3. Re-engagement Campaign

**When to use:** Launched features that have low adoption or inactive users.

**Campaign Goals:**
- Reactivate dormant or low-usage users
- Communicate missed value
- Remove adoption barriers
- Create new habits

### Template Structure

**Campaign Name:** "You're Missing Out on [Feature]"

**Example:** "Your team could save 5 hours/week with automation"

### Channels & Tactics

**Week 1: Value Reminder**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Not using [Feature] yet? Here's why you should"
  - Specific benefit relevant to user segment
  - Social proof: "[X]% of teams like yours use this"
  - Clear, simple CTA: "Try it in 2 minutes"

**Week 2: Remove Barriers**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Quick start: [Feature] in 3 clicks"
  - Address common objections or confusion
  - Simplified getting started guide
  - Video showing how easy it is

- **In-App:**
  - Contextual prompt at relevant moment
  - "Try [Feature] to [solve this specific problem]"

**Week 3: Incentive (Optional)**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Last chance: [Limited-time offer]"
  - Special incentive for trying the feature
  - Examples: extended trial, bonus credits, priority support
  - Urgency: deadline clearly stated

### Targeting & Segmentation

**Primary Segment:**
- Active users who haven't adopted this feature
- Exclude: Users who tried and explicitly rejected

**Behavior Triggers:**
- User performs action that feature could help with
- User reaches pain point feature solves
- User exhibits behavior of potential advocates

### Success Metrics

- **Activation:** % who activate feature after campaign
- **Sustained usage:** % still using 30 days later
- **Habit formation:** Average frequency of use
- **Advocacy:** % who recommend to teammates

### Example Campaign

**Feature:** Advanced Search & Filters

**Campaign:** "Find anything in seconds with Advanced Search"

**Timeline:**
- **Week 1:**
  - Email: "Still searching manually? Advanced search is 10x faster"
  - Social proof: "85% of power users rely on advanced search"

- **Week 2:**
  - Email: "Master advanced search in 3 minutes" (video)
  - In-app prompt when user performs basic search

- **Week 3:**
  - Email: "See what you can do with saved searches"
  - Case study: How [Customer] saves time

**Success Metrics:**
- 25% of targeted users try advanced search within 2 weeks
- 60% of tryers use it again within 7 days
- 40% create a saved search (habit indicator)

## 4. Upgrade/Upsell Campaign

**When to use:** Features available in higher pricing tiers or as paid add-ons.

**Campaign Goals:**
- Drive upgrades to higher plans
- Increase average revenue per user (ARPU)
- Demonstrate ROI of premium features
- Reduce churn by increasing investment

### Template Structure

**Campaign Name:** "Unlock [Advanced Feature] with [Plan]"

**Example:** "Unlock unlimited automation with Pro"

### Channels & Tactics

**Phase 1: Awareness**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "You've hit your [Free/Basic] plan limit"
  - Explain limitation with empathy
  - Showcase what's possible with upgrade
  - Case study: ROI example

- **In-App:**
  - Soft paywall when hitting limits
  - "Upgrade to unlock [specific benefit]"
  - Clear pricing and feature comparison

**Phase 2: Value Demonstration**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "See what [Premium Feature] can do"
  - Interactive demo or video
  - Customer success story
  - ROI calculator or savings estimate
  - Trial offer: "Try Pro free for 14 days"

**Phase 3: Conversion Push**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Your custom upgrade offer"
  - Personalized based on usage and needs
  - Limited-time discount (if applicable)
  - Remove friction: easy upgrade process

- **Sales Outreach (for Enterprise):**
  - Personalized call or email
  - Custom demo of advanced features
  - Pricing proposal and negotiation

### Targeting & Segmentation

**Ideal Upgrade Candidates:**
- Heavy users of free/basic tier hitting limits
- Users trying to access premium features
- Teams showing growth signals
- Users matching ideal customer profile for higher tier

**Segmentation:**
- **Hot leads:** Repeatedly hitting limits, high engagement
- **Warm leads:** Solid usage, good fit for premium
- **Future prospects:** Growing usage, early in journey

### Success Metrics

- **Upgrade rate:** % who upgrade within campaign window
- **Revenue impact:** Additional MRR/ARR generated
- **Time to upgrade:** Days from first touchpoint to conversion
- **Retention:** Upgraded user retention rate

### Example Campaign

**Feature:** Unlimited Custom Workflows (Pro Plan)

**Campaign:** "Scale your operations with unlimited workflows"

**Timeline:**
- **Trigger:** User creates 3rd workflow (approaching 5-workflow free limit)

- **Week 1:**
  - Email: "You're almost at your workflow limit"
  - Show what teams accomplish with unlimited workflows
  - Customer example: "How [Company] automated 20+ processes"

- **Week 2:**
  - Email: "Try Pro free for 14 days"
  - Interactive demo of advanced workflow features
  - ROI calculator showing time savings

- **Week 3:**
  - Email: "Your custom Pro offer"
  - Personalized based on team size
  - Limited 20% discount for first 3 months
  - Easy one-click upgrade

**In-App:**
- Banner after creating 4th workflow
- "Unlock unlimited workflows with Pro"
- Comparison table showing all Pro benefits

**Success Metrics:**
- 15% upgrade rate among users with 3+ workflows
- $50K additional MRR from campaign
- 90% retention of upgraded users at 90 days

## 5. Integration/Partnership Announcement

**When to use:** Launching integrations with third-party platforms or tools.

**Campaign Goals:**
- Create awareness among users of both platforms
- Drive integration setup and usage
- Leverage partner's audience and credibility
- Strengthen ecosystem value proposition

### Template Structure

**Campaign Name:** "[Product] now integrates with [Partner]"

**Example:** "Sync your Salesforce data seamlessly"

### Channels & Tactics

**Coordinated Launch**
- **Joint Press Release:**
  - Announce partnership and integration
  - Quotes from both companies
  - Key benefits and use cases

- **Co-Marketing Assets:**
  - Joint webinar or demo
  - Shared blog posts or case studies
  - Social media cross-promotion

**Email (Your Users)**
- **Subject:** "New: [Partner] integration is live"
- Benefits specific to integration use case
- Setup guide and video walkthrough
- Customer success story (if available)
- Clear CTA: "Connect [Partner]"

**Email (Partner's Users)**
- Similar content tailored to partner's audience
- Sent by partner through their channels
- Your company and product introduction

**In-App**
- **Integration Marketplace:**
  - Featured placement for new integration
  - "New" badge for visibility

- **Contextual Prompts:**
  - Suggest integration at relevant moments
  - "Connect [Partner] to [solve this problem]"

**Content Marketing**
- **Blog Post:** Deep dive on use cases and benefits
- **Video Tutorial:** Step-by-step setup guide
- **Case Study:** Customer success story (if available)
- **Help Center:** Documentation and troubleshooting

### Targeting & Segmentation

**Primary Segments:**
- **Your users who use partner tool:** Highest priority
- **Partner's users who could benefit from your tool:** Acquisition opportunity
- **Users looking for this integration:** Retention and satisfaction

**How to identify:**
- Survey data about tools used
- Email domain matching
- Integration wishlist voters
- Support tickets mentioning partner

### Success Metrics

- **Awareness:** Email opens, blog views, webinar attendance
- **Setup:** Integration connection rate
- **Usage:** Active integrations 30/60/90 days
- **Acquisition:** New users from partner audience
- **Satisfaction:** NPS lift among integration users

### Example Campaign

**Feature:** Slack Integration for Notifications

**Campaign:** "Stay in sync: [Product] + Slack"

**Timeline:**
- **Week 1: Launch**
  - Joint announcement blog post
  - Email to all users: "New Slack integration"
  - Social media posts from both companies
  - In-app feature spotlight

- **Week 2: Education**
  - Email: "5 ways to use [Product] in Slack"
  - Video tutorial: Setup in 2 minutes
  - Webinar: Live demo and Q&A (co-hosted with Slack)

- **Week 3: Social Proof**
  - Email: "How teams use [Product] + Slack"
  - Customer case study
  - In-app prompt for non-connectors

**Partner Co-Marketing:**
- Featured in Slack's integration directory
- Included in Slack's monthly newsletter
- Joint webinar promoted to both audiences

**Success Metrics:**
- 30% of Slack users connect integration within 30 days
- 75% of connectors still active at 60 days
- 500 new signups from Slack user base
- +15 NPS points among integration users

## 6. Deprecation/Sunset Communication

**When to use:** Removing or replacing features, changing functionality significantly.

**Campaign Goals:**
- Communicate changes clearly and early
- Minimize disruption and frustration
- Guide smooth migration to replacement
- Maintain trust and satisfaction

### Template Structure

**Campaign Name:** "Important: [Old Feature] transitioning to [New Feature]"

**Example:** "Classic editor retiring: Switch to Modern editor"

### Channels & Tactics

**Phase 1: Early Warning (8-12 weeks before)**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "Heads up: Changes coming to [Feature]"
  - Clear explanation of what's changing and why
  - Timeline with specific dates
  - Benefits of new feature
  - Migration resources available

- **In-App:**
  - Banner in old feature: "This feature will be retired on [Date]"
  - Link to migration guide

- **Blog Post:**
  - Detailed announcement
  - FAQ addressing concerns
  - Migration timeline and resources

**Phase 2: Migration Support (4-8 weeks before)**
- **Email:**
  - Subject: "[X] weeks left: Migrate to [New Feature]"
  - Step-by-step migration guide
  - Video tutorial
  - Offer: Migration assistance or support call
  - Urgency without pressure

- **In-App:**
  - Migration wizard or tool
  - Progress tracker: "3 of 5 steps complete"
  - Preview new feature alongside old

**Phase 3: Final Reminders (2-4 weeks before)**
- **Email (to non-migrated users):**
  - Subject: "Final reminder: [Old Feature] retires [Date]"
  - Urgent but helpful tone
  - One-click migration if possible
  - Support contact for help

- **In-App:**
  - More prominent warnings
  - Block creation of new content in old feature
  - Redirect to new feature with context

**Phase 4: Sunset (At deadline)**
- **Email (to remaining users):**
  - Subject: "[Old Feature] is now retired"
  - Old feature no longer accessible
  - How to access content in new feature
  - Support resources

- **In-App:**
  - Old feature removed or read-only
  - Clear messaging and next steps

**Phase 5: Post-Sunset Support (2-4 weeks after)**
- **Email:**
  - Check-in: "How's [New Feature] working for you?"
  - Additional resources or training
  - Feedback survey
  - Support offer for struggling users

### Targeting & Segmentation

**Priority Tiers:**
1. **Heavy users of old feature:** Highest impact, need most support
2. **Moderate users:** Standard migration path
3. **Light/inactive users:** Minimal communication needed

**Communication Frequency:**
- Heavy users: All phases, personalized support
- Moderate users: All phases, standard resources
- Light users: Phase 1, 3, and 4 only

### Success Metrics

- **Migration rate:** % migrated before deadline
- **Support tickets:** Volume and resolution time
- **Satisfaction:** NPS or CSAT during migration
- **Retention:** User retention through transition
- **Completion:** % successfully using new feature

### Example Campaign

**Feature:** Classic Project View → Modern Project View

**Campaign:** "Modern Project View: More powerful, easier to use"

**Timeline:**
- **Week -12:**
  - Email: "Announcement: Classic Project View retiring Dec 31"
  - Blog post explaining why and benefits of modern view
  - In-app banner with countdown

- **Week -8:**
  - Email: "Ready to migrate? Here's your step-by-step guide"
  - Video: "What's different in Modern Project View"
  - In-app: One-click migration tool launched

- **Week -6:**
  - Email (non-migrated): "Join 60% of teams already using Modern View"
  - Webinar: Live demo and Q&A
  - In-app: Preview mode showing both views side-by-side

- **Week -4:**
  - Email (heavy users, non-migrated): "Personal migration support"
  - Offer: 1-on-1 call with support team
  - In-app: Migration progress tracker

- **Week -2:**
  - Email (non-migrated): "Final reminder: 2 weeks left"
  - Urgent but empathetic tone
  - Support contact prominent

- **Week 0:**
  - Email (remaining users): "Classic View is now retired"
  - Projects automatically migrated (if safe)
  - In-app: Redirect to modern view with welcome tour

- **Week +2:**
  - Email: "How's Modern Project View working for you?"
  - Quick survey (1-2 questions)
  - Additional training resources
  - Support offer

**Success Metrics:**
- 90% migrated before deadline
- <5% increase in support tickets
- NPS remains stable (-5 points max)
- 95% retention through migration
- 80% satisfaction with new feature

## Matching Features to Campaign Types

Use this guide to choose the right campaign type:

**Is it brand new?**
→ New Feature Launch

**Does it require learning or setup?**
→ Onboarding & Education

**Is adoption low despite being available?**
→ Re-engagement

**Is it only available in higher tiers?**
→ Upgrade/Upsell

**Does it connect to another platform?**
→ Integration Announcement

**Are you replacing or removing something?**
→ Deprecation/Sunset

**Multiple apply?**
You can combine templates. Example: New integration (Launch + Integration templates)

## Customizing Templates

All templates are starting points. Customize based on:

**Your Brand Voice:**
- Formal vs casual tone
- Technical vs user-friendly language
- Conservative vs bold messaging

**Your Product:**
- Complexity level
- User sophistication
- Market positioning

**Your Resources:**
- Team size and bandwidth
- Budget for assets (video, design)
- Sales support availability

**Your Audience:**
- Industry and vertical
- Company size
- User role and expertise

The most effective campaigns combine proven patterns with authentic brand voice and deep user understanding.
